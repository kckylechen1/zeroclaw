Expand description

Did this turn succeed? The one criterion that decides whether the announcements a turn claimed stay marked delivered.

UnclaimOnDrop hands its rows back unless the turn that claimed them succeeded, and “succeeded” is spelled differently by different turn shapes: the runtime’s turns end in a Result, while the channel orchestrator ends in a three-level LlmExecutionResult whose nesting separates cancellation from timeout from tool-loop failure. Those are different answers; the question is one, and it lives here. A site cannot invent a criterion of its own without implementing this trait for its own outcome type, in the open, next to the other implementations.

That is the point. The criterion used to be hand-written at every disarm site, and with no single place being the criterion, UnclaimOnDrop’s own documentation drifted a whole version away from what those sites did — it described the rule as “the provider was reached” while every site implemented “the turn returned success” — and nothing could notice.
